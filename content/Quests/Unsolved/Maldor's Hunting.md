Quest giver: [[Maldor]]

Maldor is taking more permanent residence in orbit above the Forgotten Lands aboard the Sentinel of Serenity. Maldor is hunting for local creatures for study while he is here. He wants you to capture a wide variety of creatures for this.
## Tasks
- [ ] Capture 4 Small creatures
- [ ] Capture 5 Medium creatures
- [ ] Capture 2 Large Creatures

>[!Done] Rewards
>Maldor will offer his fabrication workshop to you to enhance your equipment should you have the pieces for doing so. You may instead choose to be paid in gold according to the CR of the creature.
