Quest giver: [[Maldor]]

Maldor is taking more permanent residence in orbit above the Forgotten Lands aboard the Sentinel of Serenity. Maldor is hunting for 3 enemies of humanity who have escaped into the area that the players are in. They are very dangerous, so when encountered, Maldor should be called from orbit to assist in capturing or killing these creatures.
## Tasks
- [ ] Find and kill [[Hatricks, Broken Blade of Oryx]]
- [ ] Find and destroy [[Vestige of Calculus]]
- [ ] Find and kill [[Orthrus, Savathun's chosen]]

>[!Done] Rewards
>For every Enemy killed, Maldor offers to enhance your weapons by a degree. Converting regular weapons to +1, +1 to +2 and so on.
